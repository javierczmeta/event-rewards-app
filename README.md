# Origami Events App 🐸

[Project Plan](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1KW6KomFtHgLSaAg0Y7VFPoCkCweqfRgp7rLaovtx7m4/edit?usp=sharing)


Origami is an events posting and discovery app where people can find whats going on in their community. It offers rewards based on the type of events you go to so you can show off your commitment to others! 🤩

![Root Page](/assets/RootPage.png)

Try it [here!](https://event-rewards-app-frontend.onrender.com/) 

---

### Features
* **User Authentication and Management**: Create a new account and save your data! Endpoints are secured so that only you can edit your events.
* **Event Exploration**: Find events happening using the multiple views available. Explore your feed and filter out the noise to find a hidden gem; use the map view to get context on event location; use the calendar view to plan your days.
![Map Page](/assets/MapPage.png)
* **Find other users**: See what others are up to and the events they have organized!
* **Rewards**: Get rewards for going to events. The smaller and more intimate, the more rewards you get. These can be used to obtain badges to display in your profile so that everyone sees your commitment.
![Rewards](/assets/Rewards.png)
* **RSVP and Attendance Tracking**: mark the events you plan to go to and check in to them! This helps keep tabs on attendees and your friends!
* **Recommendation System**: If you don't know wheare to go, check out the tailored recommendations based on your history, other users who share your interests, and the distance to the event.
* **Scheduling**: Have a lot of events in one day and don't know where to go? The scheduling page can help you plan out your day to maximize (or minimize) different properties. Also, it can take into account commute times so you can be on time to every event!
![Schedule](/assets/Schedule.png)
* **Make your voice Heard**: You can leave reviews on events after you have checked in to them to leave feedback for the organizer and other users who want to go to that event!
